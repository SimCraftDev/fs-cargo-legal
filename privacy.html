FS Cargo Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 10, 2026

Overview

FS Cargo is a companion application for Microsoft Flight Simulator. This page explains what information FS Cargo collects, how it is used, and your choices, including the optional SMS text messaging feature.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: email address and basic profile information when you create an FS Cargo account.
  • Phone number: only if you choose to interact with the FS Cargo SMS text service by sending a message to (317) 779-2908.
  • Message content: the text of messages you send to and receive from FS Cargo's SMS number, stored so you can view your message history in the app.
  • Flight and simulator data: information read from Microsoft Flight Simulator, such as aircraft position, fuel, and weather at your location, used only to answer your questions and power in-app features.
  • Device information: a non-reversible device identifier used solely to prevent account or subscription sharing, for accounts where this protection is enabled.

How We Use Information

We use the information above only to operate FS Cargo's features: responding to your text messages, syncing your account and flight data across sessions, and preventing unauthorized account sharing.

What We Don't Do

We do not share, sell, or otherwise provide your mobile phone number or messaging consent information to any third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. We do not send unsolicited or marketing text messages. Every SMS reply is triggered by a message you send first.

SMS Messaging

  • Message frequency varies based on your activity.
  • Message and data rates may apply.
  • Reply STOP at any time to opt out of text messages. Reply START to opt back in. Reply HELP for support.
  • You may also disable the feature in the app or block the number through your phone carrier.

Data Retention

Message history and account data are retained for as long as your account is active, so you can review your history in the app. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.
